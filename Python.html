Python EDA Projects

Click on the image to access the PDF of the relevant study.

Stroke Analysis

Missing data, variable types and distributions on the Stroke dataset were examined in detail; especially the interactions of factors such as age, gender and medical history were analyzed with visualizations. The study presents a comprehensive exploratory analysis process to understand the data structure before modeling.

Laptop Price Analysis

Variables affecting prices were analyzed on the laptop dataset. The effects of RAM, CPU brand, SSD capacity and screen resolution on prices were visualized. Outlier cleaning and categorical variable transformations were performed.

Bike Sharing Analysis

The factors affecting the daily rental number in the bike sharing system were analyzed. The effects of environmental variables such as weather, temperature, humidity on demand were visualized, and the usage density was examined on an hourly basis. Time series features and correlation structure were extracted..

Diabetes Analysis

The effects of variables such as glucose, insulin, BMI and age on diabetes risk were examined. Missing data control, correlation analysis and feature distribution according to the target variable were visualized.

Sleep Health Analysis

The relationship between sleep patterns and health was examined, the effects of sleep duration and quality on metabolic health, immune system and mental functions were analyzed. Correlations were extracted by cleaning missing data, and the connections between sleep problems and chronic disease risks were visualized.

Netflix Trend Analysis

Netflix user data was analyzed to examine viewing habits, content preferences and subscription behaviors. Viewer demographics and hourly usage density were determined, and findings that would contribute to popular genres and content recommendation systems were visualized.

Student Performance Analysis

The relationships between exam results and demographic and social factors were analyzed on student performance data. The effects of study habits, family support, and school environment on achievement were examined, and key inferences for performance prediction were visualized.

Supermarket Analysis

Product-based revenue, seasonal trends and regional sales differences were analyzed on supermarket sales data. Campaign effects and best-selling categories were determined and implications for stock management and marketing strategies were visualized.

Health Expenditure Analysis

Individuals' health expenditures were analyzed and the effects of age, BMI, insurance status and chronic diseases on costs were examined. Distribution analyses, group comparisons and basic regression-based inferences were made on the data.

Real Estate Analysis

Intensive data cleaning operations were performed on the housing market data for 2001–2022. Missing values, inconsistent formats, and outliers were edited; then periodic price changes, the impact of economic fluctuations, and regional trends were analyzed. Reliable trend inferences and visualizations were made with the structure obtained after cleaning.
