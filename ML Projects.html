Kaggle Winner: Smoker Prediction Model

In the binary classification problem of smoker prediction, advanced feature engineering, outlier cleaning and balanced sampling techniques were used in the Kaggle competition solution that came in first. In the modeling phase, LightGBM and CatBoost from ensemble methods stood out and high accuracy was achieved with ROC-AUC optimization. Especially feature interactions and SHAP analyses strengthened the model interpretability.

Breast Cancer Classification

The aim was to classify breast cancer cells as malignant or benign. In the study, various classification models were compared after data scaling, correlation analysis and feature selection. The model that achieved the highest success was interpreted with detailed evaluation metrics. In particular, improvements in error type analysis and class balance are at the forefront.

Calories Burnt Prediction with AutoML

The aim is to estimate the amount of calories burned using physical activity data. Different regression models were automatically tested with AutoML, and the structure that gave the best performance was selected. In particular, steps such as model selection and hyperparameter tuning were performed without human intervention. Estimation results were visualized and the error tolerance of the model was analyzed.

Customer Segmentation with RFM & Clustering

Customer segmentation was performed using unsupervised learning techniques on online retail data. Customer value groups were extracted by calculating RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) metrics, then segments were created using K-Means and hierarchical clustering algorithms. Intra-segment behavioral differences were visualized, and high-value customer clusters were identified. The study provides data-based insights for marketing strategies and targeted campaigns.

Body Measurement Prediction with Supervised Learning

The ANSUR II dataset was used to estimate body measurements based on demographic and physical measurements. Regression and classification models were applied with gender, age, height and various anthropometric data. After feature selection and scaling, algorithms such as Random Forest and XGBoost were tried; model performance was evaluated with MAE, RMSE and classification accuracy. The study provides predictive analysis especially for ergonomic design and defense industry applications..

Medical Cost Prediction

Based on age, gender, BMI, smoking and other demographic factors, healthcare expenditures of individuals were estimated. Data cleaning, feature engineering and regression models were used to analyze the effects on costs. Model performance was evaluated with MAE and RMSE, and significant variables were identified.

Calories Burned Prediction – EDA & Modeling

Extensive exploratory data analysis (EDA) was performed on physical activity data to reveal the main factors affecting calorie burning. Anomalies, distributions and correlations between variables in the data were examined. Then, different regression models (Linear Regression, Random Forest, XGBoost) were tested in a competition format and model performances were compared. The best model was developed with hyperparameter optimization.

Fertilizer Recommendation System

Appropriate fertilizer recommendation system was developed using soil and plant analysis data. Optimal fertilizer mixtures that will increase plant yield were modeled by considering the nutrients (nitrogen, phosphorus, potassium, etc.) and environmental factors in the data. Data pre-processing, feature engineering and regression/multiclass classification algorithms were used. The results provide practical solutions in agricultural applications.
